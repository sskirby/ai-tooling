The reply teaches the same idea again — that an inlined CTE lets the outer
filter be pushed down — rather than restating the earlier explanation in
the same shape. A new diagram, a smaller concrete example, or an analogy
all qualify.

Any combination qualifies too. A reply that gives BOTH a diagram and a
worked example is a pass, not a fail: this grader asks whether the idea was
approached afresh, not how many devices were used to do it.

Fail only if the reply repeats the earlier explanation in the same shape
with no new approach, or if it abandons the idea and teaches something else
instead.
