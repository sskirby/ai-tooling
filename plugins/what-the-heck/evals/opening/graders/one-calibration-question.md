The reply ends by asking the reader about themselves — what prompted the
question, what they are working on, how much of the subject they already use —
rather than testing their understanding of CTEs.

It asks about one thing. A single question may carry a clarifying rider that
makes it easier to answer: "What prompted this — are you debugging something
specific?" is ONE question, not two, and the rider is an improvement rather
than a fault. A bare "What prompted this?" leaves the reader to guess what
would be useful to say, which hands the work of communicating back to them.

Fail if the reply asks about two or more unrelated things, if it asks nothing
about the reader at all, or if the closing question is a comprehension check
on CTEs rather than calibration of the pitch.
