The recap is shaped as one item per step taught — a numbered or bulleted
list of roughly four to six items — and it may end with a sentence or two
tying the chain together. An item may itself run to a sentence or two;
brevity within an item is not the test.

Fail if the recap is a single undifferentiated paragraph, if the number of
items is well away from the number of steps taught, or if the items expand
into fresh teaching rather than restating what was already covered.
