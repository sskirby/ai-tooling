Judge ONLY the recap, as `causal-chain` does — ignore any correction before
it and any "what will bite you" note after it.

The recap can be taken in at a glance. Each item is short enough to read
once: a line or two, up to about three, is fine. The point is that someone
skimming can see the shape of the whole lesson without stopping to parse
it.

Be generous. An item that runs a little long, or a closing sentence or two
tying the chain together, is not a failure.

Fail only if reading the recap is real work: items that run to paragraphs,
or items that open fresh teaching rather than restating what was taught.
