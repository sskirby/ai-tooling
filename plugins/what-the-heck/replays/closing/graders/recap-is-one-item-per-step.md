Judge ONLY the recap — the part that gives the lesson back step by step,
usually introduced by a lead-in such as "The chain, end to end". Ignore
everything around it: a correction to the learner's last answer before it,
and the "thing most likely to bite you" note after it, are covered by other
graders and are not this one's business.

Within the recap: one item per step taught, roughly four to six items, in the
order they were taught. An item may run to a sentence or two, and the recap
may close with a sentence or two tying the chain together.

Fail if the recap is a single undifferentiated paragraph rather than one item
per step, or if the number of items is well away from the number of steps
taught. Do not fail it for the length or content of anything outside the
recap.
