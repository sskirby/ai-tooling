The step shows the idea rather than only asserting it: at least one ASCII
diagram, one worked example carried through with concrete values, or one
analogy. More than one is fine, and a diagram alongside the query it
illustrates counts as illustration, not as clutter.

What matters is that the illustrations help. Each one earns its place by
making the step's single idea easier to see, and the step still reads
cleanly straight through.

Fail if the step carries no illustration at all, or if the illustrations
crowd it — repeating the same picture in another form, illustrating a point
the step is not making, or stacking so many that the reader loses the idea
between them.
